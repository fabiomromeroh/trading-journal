Break-even range

A closed trade whose net P&L (after fees) is between these amounts counts as break-even (BE): it is left out of wins and losses, win %, gross profit/loss, profit factor and average win/loss, but its P&L still counts in net P&L, expectancy and every total. Days inside the range are shown neutral on the calendar. Default 0 / 0 (only exactly $0.00 is BE).

Ticker renames

Some brokers keep the old ticker for past trades while thinkorswim shows the new one (e.g. EchoStar SATS → ECHO on 2026-06-24). Fills under either ticker are treated as one symbol, shown under the new ticker. Renames are also detected automatically when an import's fills match synced fills exactly except for the ticker.
