Break-even range

A closed trade whose net P&L (after fees) is between these amounts counts as break-even (BE): it is left out of wins and losses, win %, gross profit/loss, profit factor and average win/loss, but its P&L still counts in net P&L, expectancy and every total. Days inside the range are shown neutral on the calendar. Default 0 / 0 (only exactly $0.00 is BE).
