How to use it
  1. Pick the period / filters above (the summary follows them).
  2. Press Copy prompt + data, open any AI chat, paste, send.
  3. Ask follow-ups: “which single rule would have saved me the most?”, “compare my best and worst setup”.

Tip: the 5-10 most useful things you can add are in the notes boxes of each trade (what went wrong, emotions, lesson): the AI quotes your own words back to you.

Possible later option: if you ever get an API key, the app could request this review automatically. Not built; nothing is called today.
