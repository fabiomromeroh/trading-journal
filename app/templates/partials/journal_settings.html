Journal, stops & R levels

Defaults for the trade page. A stop you type on a trade always wins.

Stocks only. Long stop = lowest low of the regular-session 5-minute bars from 09:30 NY up to and including the bar of your FIRST entry fill, minus the buffer (shorts: highest high plus buffer). No 5-minute data (older than ~58 days, or no fill time): the daily low of the entry day is used and flagged “approx”. Initial risk $ = (first entry − stop) × first entry size. Options have no default stop.

Changing the buffer or premarket recomputes all auto stops; manual stops are never touched.

Shows old → new for every auto stop first (dry run), then you apply. Stops computed from 5-minute bars are stored with the trade and survive the provider's ~60-day window. Manual stops and Risk $ are never touched. Runs automatically for new trades after syncs.
