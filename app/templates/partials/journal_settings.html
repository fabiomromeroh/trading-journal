Journal, stops & R levels

Defaults for the trade page. A stop you type on a trade always wins.

Stocks only: the stop is the day's low (shorts: high) of the New York entry date, from the daily bar, marked “auto”. Risk $ = (avg entry − stop) × max position size. Options have no default stop.
